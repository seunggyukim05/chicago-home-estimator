A few things to have handy.

Every home has details a model can’t see. Use the estimate alongside a review of the home’s condition and suitable recent sales before deciding on a listing price.

BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Know where your estimate comes from.

We built this student project to help agents and buyers make sense of Chicago property records. Here’s what goes into the estimate and where it needs a closer look.

How the estimate works

The model uses building and lot area, home age, bedrooms, full and half bathrooms, property class, ZIP, and date-based features. It predicts log sale price and converts the result to dollars. The exported smearing factor is 1.0.

Where the data come from

Recorded sales

Cook County transaction records, September 1, 2019–August 11, 2026.

Mortgage rate source ↗
Average error is a performance measure across evaluated sales, not a prediction interval for a specific property. Sparse locations, missing condition details, exterior area measurements, and future market changes can affect usefulness.

Sale dates are recording dates and may differ from closing dates. Market summaries describe the supplied sample. The model does not establish causal price effects or an optimal asking price.
