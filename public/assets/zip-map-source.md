# Chicago ZIP map source

City of Chicago — Boundaries, ZIP Codes.
Source: https://data.cityofchicago.org/d/unjd-c2ca
Downloaded October 21, 2026 from https://data.cityofchicago.org/resource/unjd-c2ca.geojson?$limit=1000

59 published features. Local file retains ZIP identifiers and geometry rounded to five decimal places. Areas are approximate, not individual property locations or current USPS delivery boundaries. Supported ZIPs outside the city map remain selectable. The underlying Cook County transaction records and model have not changed.

No map tiles, paid APIs, or keys are used.
